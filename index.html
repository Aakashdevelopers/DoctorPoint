App Screens & Modules

A complete breakdown of the Doctor Point Android application screens.

1. Authentication & Signup

Splash screen, secure patient sign-in, account registration, patient profile creation, and saved preferences.

2. Home Dashboard

State location picker, promotional banner slider, quick access grid, top-rated doctor list, and notification bell.

3. Doctor Finder & Filters

Explore fragment with category chips, live query search bar, sorting dialog, and multi-parameter doctor filter.

4. Doctor Profile & Calendar

Qualifications, clinic details, gallery slider, Google Maps directions, reviews tab, and interactive day/time slot picker.

5. Booking & Confirmation

Patient details input, consultation fee summary, instant slot reservation, and booking confirmation.

6. Appointments & Medical Vault

Tabbed appointment cards, instant reschedule or cancellation, rate doctor dialog, plus medical record uploader & viewer.
